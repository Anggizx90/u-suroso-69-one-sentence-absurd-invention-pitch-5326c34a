Pitch a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance, in exactly one sentence. The funniest, cleverest, most original pitch wins.

Our entry — the Doorway Parrot, against walking into a room and forgetting why:

> The Doorway Parrot perches on the doorframe and repeats whatever you muttered in the hallway, so the moment you walk in and forget why, it squawks your own errand back at you.

175 characters, one sentence. The joke is the invention itself: a parrot — a bird famous for mindless repetition — stationed at the exact doorway where your memory drops the errand, handing your own muttered plan back at the moment it evaporates. Chosen from 14 scored candidates (runners-up: a matchmaking service that pairs every container with its one true lid; a pocket referee who officiates the last fry).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Writes the pitch to `out/entry.txt` (the grader's `/output/entry.txt`).
